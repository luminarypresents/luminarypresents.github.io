Luminary Presents LLC

Attendance & contact

Luminary Presents creates immersive installations at American landmarks and cultural institutions, telling America’s story through music and live discussions.

Invitation-only, free attendance

Our events are financed by sponsors. Guests attend by invitation and free of charge. We do not publicly sell tickets, charge admission, or take reservations through this website.

Sponsorship and patronage

We welcome conversations with potential sponsors and patrons interested in helping shape America’s future through unique artistic programming. To explore being part of an event, get in touch with us.

Contact and support

For company information, sponsorship inquiries, or questions related to an invitation, please email contact@luminarypresents.com.

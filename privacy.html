Privacy policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy describes how Luminary Presents LLC handles information related to this company website and inquiries sent to us.

Information you share with us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if provided, the contents of your message, and any other information you choose to share. We use this information to respond to your inquiry, communicate with you, and manage the relationship or request you contact us about.

Website technology

This website has no contact form, account registration, advertising trackers, or analytics scripts. It does not set cookies or use browser storage. Its fonts and artwork do not require third-party requests. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and secure the website.

Email and hosting providers

Our email and website hosting providers may process information as needed to provide those services. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

Payments

This website does not collect payment-card details or process payments. Please do not send payment-card information by email.

Retention and privacy requests

We retain correspondence as needed to handle inquiries, maintain business records, and meet applicable legal obligations. To ask about information you have shared, or to request access, correction, or deletion, email contact@luminarypresents.com. We will consider requests subject to applicable law and any recordkeeping obligations.
